# Literature review (sprint agent, 2026-06-09)

Key conclusions:
1. **Novelty of the factorization question**: No prior proof, no-go, or counterexample found for whether the electron's double-valued corep factorizes as (nontrivial single-valued boson rep) x (double-valued fermion corep) over 2D wallpaper groups (or 3D SGs) with spinful TRS. Closest precedents: parton band-rep tech for neutral spinons (Song-He-Vishwanath-Wang 1811.11182), bosonic charge+crystal fractionalization (Manjunath-Barkeshli 2005.10265), glide fractionalization for bosons on p4g (Lee-Hermele-Parameswaran 1605.08042). Harden via citation sweep of 1811.11182 and 2309.15118 before claiming novelty.
2. **PSG context**: chargons in nontrivial *projective* space-group reps are standard in PSG / symmetric-fractionalization literature (Wen cond-mat/0107071; Essin-Hermele 1212.0593; Sachdev-Scheurer pseudogap SU(2) gauge theory 1711.09925). LSM-anomaly matching forces nontrivial fractionalization classes (Zaletel-Vishwanath 1410.2894; Cheng et al. 1511.02263). Xu-Wu-Ye-Luo-Jian-Xu 2106.14910: chargon sector itself subject to LSM at half filling.
3. **SG135**: dichotomy (band insulator needs nu=8n, interacting symmetric insulator allowed at nu=4) from Watanabe-Po-Vishwanath-Zaletel 1505.04193 + 1603.05646; double Dirac from Wieder-Kim-Rappe-Kane 1512.00074; ONLY existing interacting attack is Manning-Coe & Bradlyn 2306.00221 (HK). Slave-boson route untaken (citation scan of 2306.00221: only HK-methodology citers).
4. **KMH slave-boson paper**: correct arXiv ID **1107.0007** (PRB 84, 235149). No erratum found. CAVEAT for write-up: its half-filling SL window is contradicted by exact QMC (Sorella-Otsuka-Yunoki 1207.1783; Hohenadler-Assaad review 1211.1774) - the SL sliver is a mean-field artifact. Our reproduction validates the MFT solver, not the physics of the sliver.
