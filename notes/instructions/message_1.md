### Formatting

1. When writing an executive summary, a good format is to explain, in as simple terms as possible, the 2-5 key findings. Ideally each finding in the summary is supported by a single graph which has very straightforward axes such that someone seeing that graph would immediately understand why this is relevance. (Maybe a good test of this would be to try to a fresh agent explain what the point of the graph alone is, with no context. If they can't then its maybe not clear enough!)
